# Supplied research

This folder preserves **15 supplied documents** and **three existing text extractions** used during Floatie's development. The originals keep their filenames, directory structure, attribution, and bytes. The [source manifest](source-manifest.json) records their original locations, sizes, and SHA256 hashes.

Start with our [original research review](../performance/research-20261011-local-reconciliation.json), [added-source review](../performance/research-20261011-added-sources-reconciliation.json), and [attempts and results](../performance/ATTEMPTS-AND-RESULTS.md). These connect the reports to the implemented engine and measured experiments. The supplied reports contain proposals and historical assumptions; their speed projections are not measurements of Floatie.
