Laguna S 2.1 Local Inference Optimization Report
Hardware: RTX 4060 Ti 16GB + Threadripper 1950X + 128GB DDR4-2133
Executive Summary
Current performance at 8k context: 779 tok/s prefill, 16.1 tok/s generation. Targets: 900 tok/s prefill and 30 tok/s generation.
30 tok/s generation is plausible if expert-cache hit rate reaches ≥85–90% and residual misses are overlapped or cheapened. The current 16.1 tok/s indicates the GPU is frequently waiting on CPU experts or PCIe uploads.
Highest-leverage path: (1) adaptive expert cache with high hit rate, (2) overlap residual GPU cache uploads with CPU work, (3) multi-token Zen-1 AVX2 kernels, (4) compact SWA KV so VRAM remains available for experts.
1. Architecture Comparison
Key Facts
Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: 125B/6B active, GDN + QSA hybrid, large expert pool, MTP draft. Strata is specialized for this model.
Laguna S 2.1: 118B/~8B active, 256 routed experts (top-10) + 1 shared, 12 global + 36 SWA-512 layers, softplus gating, GQA, DFlash draft.
Strata speed comes from adaptive VRAM expert cache, concurrent CPU computation of misses, prefill streaming, and model-specific kernels.
What transfers: expert cache idea, CPU/GPU overlap, prefill streaming, verification batching concept. What does not: attention kernels and exact quant kernels.
Feasibility of 30 tok/s
Plausible with high hit rate (≥85–90%), good overlap, and solid DFlash acceptance (~4–6). Hardware ceilings are Zen-1 AVX2 throughput and the measured 6.6 GB/s PCIe link.
2. CPU Experts and GPU Cache Uploads
CPU gate/up and down dominate the miss path. Worker overhead is negligible (~0.26%).
Implement multi-token Zen-1 AVX2 kernels for the actual mixed quant formats that miss most often.
Experiment with moving GPU cache-refresh uploads to a separate stream so they overlap CPU work (after GPU has finished reading cached weights).
Measure carefully: overlap only helps if the upload was previously on the critical path.
3. Sustaining Speed at 128k
Global-layer KV grows linearly and competes with expert-cache VRAM. SWA layers (36 of 48) stay bounded by the 512-token window if compact allocation is used.
Required: compact/ring SWA KV, efficient attention kernels, chunked prefill, and explicit VRAM trade-off between KV and expert cache.

Strata adaptive tier: decay routing counts (default ×0.7) every few windows and swap highest-count missing experts into VRAM. Measured gains: misses −31%, hit rate 90.8% → 93.4%.
Least-Stale: prefer evicting stale (previous-pass) experts, then far-layer experts. Directly attacks layer-sequential collisions.
6. Recommended Measurement Plan
On the identical 8k request, measure for each change: expert-cache hit rate, CPU expert time, upload time (CUDA events), DFlash acceptance length, and wall-clock tok/s. These numbers decide whether 30 tok/s is reachable.
