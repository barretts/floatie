# Evidence packaging

The JSON measurements and deployment receipts preserve their original contents and historical local paths. Markdown navigation is adapted for this repository. `installed-evidence/path-map.json` maps selected historical installed logs/receipts to bundled copies. The 336-file original inventory and 98 saved inference measurements describe their original capture scope; later repair and publication artifacts are separate.

External research originals/extractions and temporary tools were omitted. References to those materials are labeled local and unbundled. Archived source candidates remain historical and are not the retained engine. No inference requests or tests ran during packaging.
